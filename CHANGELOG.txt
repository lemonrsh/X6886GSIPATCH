v11.2.2 (reliability fixes)
* FOD HBM: watchdog timeouts are token-gated and cancelled/reaped before a new HBM session, so an old timer cannot turn off a newer fingerprint glow. MAX_S and the battery-temperature cutoff are unchanged.
* FOD/SOFOD: fod_service.sh and sofod_touch.sh now use the shared single-instance guard; lib.sh ignores stale or unrelated PIDs. Removed the broad pkill from sofod_touch.sh. Reboot after an in-place update so pre-guard helpers stop.
* Performance: background dexopt may run during AOD/Dozing as well as Asleep, still gated by charging or battery >= 40%. Failed job attempts retry instead of marking the version complete.
* CI: host-side regression tests and bash/dash syntax checks run on PRs/main and before release. No wake-order, PROP_WAIT, governor, HAL patch, or refresh-rate changes.
